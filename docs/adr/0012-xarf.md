# ADR-012: XARF Abuse Reports

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Automated XARF abuse reports dispatched to hosting/cloud Trust & Safety teams
(AWS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Cloudflare) for confirmed compromised hosts.

## Decision
- `abuse.ShouldDispatch` gates dispatch: only ssh/http_persona/raw_tcp
  modules, never port_scan types, requires evidence (payload SHA-256 or IOCs
  or canary token).
- `abuse.BuildReport` creates XARF 1.0 report with reporter, incident, and
  evidence (no raw payloads, only hashes/IOCs).
- `abuse.Dispatcher` POSTs reports to configured endpoints with retry-free
  single attempt; maintains in-memory dispatch record.

## Consequences
- Single-packet port scans never generate XARF reports (legal guardrail).
- Evidence-backed reporting only; no PII or secret leakage.
- Dispatch failures logged but don't block event processing.
